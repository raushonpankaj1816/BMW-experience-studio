Origin & Ownership

Bayerische Motoren Werke.

Who BMW is, where it came from, and who owns it today.

BMW began as an aircraft-engine builder in Munich, became a motorcycle manufacturer in 1923, and only started building cars in 1928 with the licensed Dixi. Today the BMW Group builds vehicles on three continents and sells them in more than 140 countries.

It remains an independent, publicly listed company — not owned by any other carmaker. Its headquarters is the four-cylinder BMW Tower designed by Karl Schwanzer, opened in 1973 beside the Olympiapark in Munich.
