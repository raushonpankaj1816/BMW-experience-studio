Person Journey

How a Bavarian engine maker survived a century.

BMW survived two world wars, the loss of its Eisenach factory, near-bankruptcy in 1959 and decades of doubt about whether it should build cars at all. What held it together was a simple habit: engineering the next car to drive better than the last one.

The kidney grille arrived by accident in 1934, the motorsport department arrived out of necessity in 1972, and the electric i brand arrived before most rivals had a prototype. Each era on this timeline solved a problem the previous one left behind.

  • 1916Founded in Munich as an aero-engine builder
  • 1923First motorcycle, the R32 boxer twin
  • 1928First car, the licensed Dixi
  • 1959Rescued by the Quandt family — still the anchor shareholder
  • 1972BMW Motorsport GmbH founded in Munich
  • 2013i3 — the first carbon-fibre production BMW
  • TodayIndependent, publicly listed, ~46.7% held by the Quandt family
