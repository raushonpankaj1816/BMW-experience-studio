Six pillars

What makes a modern BMW.

From the screen in front of you to the cells under the floor.

Interface

BMW Curved Display

A single pane of glass carries a 12.3-inch driver display and a 14.9-inch control display. BMW Operating System 8 and above adds QuickSelect, so functions open without leaving the map.

Electric

Fifth-generation eDrive

Motor, power electronics and transmission merged into one housing, with magnets free of rare earths. The result: more range from the same battery, and power that arrives before you finish the thought.

Architecture

Neue Klasse

An 800-volt platform, Gen6 round battery cells with materially higher energy density, 400 kW charging and a completely new electronic architecture — the blueprint for every BMW built after 2025.

Traction

xDrive all-wheel drive

Electronically controlled, rear-biased and fast enough to shift torque before the inside wheel slips. On most models the AWD system can decouple entirely for rear-drive efficiency on a cruise.

Efficiency

48V mild hybrid

A starter-generator embedded in the transmission recovers energy under braking, boosts torque off the line and lets the engine switch off while coasting — efficiency without a plug.

Design

Surface language

Flush door handles, a near-vertical kidney grille and precisely drawn light signatures. Aerodynamic detail is not decoration — every millimetre of Cd is range.
