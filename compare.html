Reading the table

Three numbers that matter most.

Power Peak output in horsepower. Electric models deliver all of it from zero revs, so they feel stronger than the number suggests.
Drive Rear-wheel for balance, xDrive for all-weather traction, front-wheel on the compact UKL/FAAR cars.
0-100 km/h The launch figure — the clearest single measure of how the car leaves a standstill.
